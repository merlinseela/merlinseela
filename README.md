# Merlin Seela [🦅](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merlin_(bird))
### Engineer on the Edge of Software and Hardware

I learned programming the old-fashioned way, with time, passion and a good bucket of self-inflicted headaches. Stack Overflow rescued me more often than LLMs did. Digital Twins became my personal excitement when I was first introduced to them at [Fraunhofer IPK](https://www.ipk.fraunhofer.de/) in Berlin. Working on Asset Administration Shells as early as `AAS V2` and `BaSyx V1` was a great but also painful experience. Today I focus on clear governance of source code, left-shifting principles and robust and scalable architectures by design. I favor Domain-Driven Design and clear separation of concerns within the codebase while maintaining time-to-value focus through rapid prototyping and practical compromises whenever necessary. 

```markdown
**Technical debt is not something we can avoid but reduce.**
```

## 🏗️ Current Mission:
I am currently designing and developing [saftch](https://github.com/merlinseela/i40-saftch), a Digital Twin powered testing framework for hardware designs utilizing stateless daemon services on the targeted hardware.

- **Architecture:** N:(N):N multiplicity (Developer/Producer <-> Intelligent FastAPI-Proxy wrapping (AAS/BaSyx) <-> Target Hardware Fleet).
- **Network Optimization:** Edge-driven payload reduction via lightweight Digital Twin Models, explicit-compounding identifiers, batching of edge-logs, and more.
- **Deployment:** Idempotent and fully automated one-touch provisioning for edge devices. (as far as it can be pushed!)
